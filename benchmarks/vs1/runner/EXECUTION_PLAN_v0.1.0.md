## 2. Statistical design (Solomon-equivalent freeze)

```text
PRIMARY ENDPOINT:      final_task_quality (0..1, fraction of hidden tests passed)
PRIMARY COMPARISON:    E vs B, E vs D, F vs E (paired within replicate)
INDEPENDENCE STRUCTURE: family = replicate (fresh source per replicate)
                       arms share the source WITHIN a replicate (declared;
                       source reuse across replicates is pseudoreplication
                       and is NOT done)
SAMPLE SIZE:           R = 3 replicates
PRECISION CRITERION:   paired 95% CI width on primary effect ≈ 0.30 with
                       SD≈0.3 per cell mean → pilot-inferential, not
                       confirmatory (recorded; mirrors EE-2B PILOT_INFERENTIAL)
FAILURE TOLERANCE:     method-failure rate ≤ 5% (frozen)
MISSING DATA:          method failures counted as unsuccessful outcomes;
                       reported separately; never imputed
EFFECT-SIZE REPORTING: means + paired 95% CI (Wald) per contrast
MULTIPLE COMPARISONS:  primary comparisons frozen as 3 (E-vs-B, E-vs-D, F-vs-E);
                       no exploratory testing without reporting
SEEDS:                 n/a for LLM calls (temperature 0); deterministic
                       scheduling seed recorded in EXECUTION_SCHEDULE.json
```

R derivation (precision, not power): at pilot scale the meaningful delta for ThinkOS is the EE-2B threshold family (δ=0.25). With R=3 per cell and cell SD≈0.30, the paired CI width on a 3-pair contrast is wide (~0.7). Pooling across the 6 conditions for the arm-level mean gives N=18 per arm → SE(mean diff) ≈ 0.30/√18 ≈ 0.07 → CI width ≈ 0.28. **R=3 yields an arm-level precision of ≈ ±0.15, adequate to resolve δ=0.25 pilot-inferential effects.** Frozen: **R = 3**, 3 replicates per arm×condition.
